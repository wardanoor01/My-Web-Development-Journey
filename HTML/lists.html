Steps to Bake a Cake

  1. Preheat the oven to 350° (175°C).
  2. Mix the dry ingredients.
  3. Whisk the eggs, milk, and vanilla extract.
  4. Combine wet and dry ingredients.
  5. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 30 minutes.
  6. Let the cake cool for serving.
